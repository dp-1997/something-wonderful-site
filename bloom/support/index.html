Bloom support

Bloom is a clay sunflower who reflects the shape of your day. If something isn't right, or you'd like to say hello, write to hello@damianpickett.com. A person reads every message.

How does the plant get water?

From the shape of your day: steps, workouts, sleep, mindful minutes and time in daylight from Apple Health, plus time spent with him through Water. He never dies; a quiet day only makes him a little thirsty.

I said no to Health or Screen Time. Can I change my mind?

Yes. Open your iPhone's Settings, then Privacy & Security. Health access is under Health, then Bloom. Screen Time access is under Screen Time. Bloom's own Settings, the cog on the garden, can take you there too.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold your Home Screen or Lock Screen, choose Edit, then Add Widget, and pick Bloom.

Where is my data?

Only on your iPhone. Bloom has no account and no server. You can export a copy of everything from Bloom's Settings. Read the privacy policy for the details.

Does Bloom work on iPhone Duo?

Yes. Folded, the garden fits the outer screen; open, Planty and his words sit side by side like a picture book, in either orientation.
