Bloom keeps your day on your phone

Bloom is a clay sunflower who reflects the shape of your day. Everything he knows stays on your iPhone.

What Bloom reads, and why

With your permission, Bloom reads a few things from Apple Health: steps, workouts, sleep, mindful minutes and time in daylight. They water the plant. Bloom only reads; it never adds to or changes your Health records.

With your permission, Bloom uses Apple's Screen Time to notice when the apps you choose have run past the limit you set. Apple never tells Bloom which apps they are or what you do in them, only that a limit was crossed.

If you save a moment, Bloom keeps the photo you choose or take, and any words you write, in its own folder on your iPhone. Bloom uses the camera only when you ask it to take a photo, and never records sound.

Where it goes

Nowhere. Bloom has no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code that collects data. Nothing you share with the plant is sold, shared or sent to anyone, including us. Your Health information is never used for advertising or marketing.

The plant, his journal, your photos and his memory live only on your iPhone, in Bloom's own storage and the widget it shares with your Home Screen and Lock Screen, and in any copy you choose to export from Settings.

The daily song

Each day the plant can share a song. Tapping Spotify or YouTube opens that service with the song; from then on, that service's own privacy policy applies. Bloom does not tell either service anything about you, and it does not know whether you listened.

Notifications

If you allow them, Bloom's notifications are scheduled on your iPhone itself. Nothing is sent from a server.

Your choices

  • Every permission can be changed at any time in your iPhone's Settings.
  • Bloom's Settings can export a copy of everything it keeps, and can forget the song history.
  • Deleting Bloom deletes the plant and everything it kept.

Changes

If what Bloom does with your information ever changes, this page will say so first, with a new date below.
